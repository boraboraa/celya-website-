Frequently asked questions

Your questions, straight answers

How much does it cost?
Less than a secretary, more than voicemail. The price depends on your call volume: after a short conversation you get a clear proposal, with no hidden fees.
Is it legal in Belgium?
Yes. Janet does one thing only: answering the calls your business receives. She says she is an AI, and your data is hosted in Europe, in line with the GDPR.
What does she sound like?
Like a person on the phone: a natural voice, a real conversation. The easiest way is to judge for yourself — call +32 460 25 44 13.
How long does setup take?
A few days. We prepare Janet with your information — opening hours, services, frequent questions — then you simply forward your calls to her. You keep your number.
Which languages does she speak?
French and Dutch first, and English too. She answers in the caller's language.
What if she doesn't know the answer?
She never makes things up. If a question goes beyond her information, she takes a message, tells the caller you will call back, and passes the request on to you — flagged urgent if it is urgent.
How is this different from voicemail?
Voicemail records — and most people hang up before the beep. Janet holds a real conversation: the appointment is booked or the message taken during the call. Your customer is helped right away, and doesn't call a competitor.
Does she really book appointments?
Yes. She offers your free slots and books during the call: the appointment appears directly in your calendar.
Is my data protected?
Yes. Calls, transcripts and call summaries are hosted in Europe and remain your property. Your customers can request access or deletion at any time, as the GDPR provides.
