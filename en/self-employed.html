01The callback that never happens

One missed call, two people waiting

The phone buzzes in the middle of a job. You do not pick up, and the caller leaves no message. Later, an unknown number on the screen — no name, no reason, no idea whether it was urgent. So both sides make the same decision, in silence.

You I'll call them back. already on the next job
Both
waiting
The caller He'll call me back. no message left

Nobody dials. In the evening it is too late to disturb anyone; the next day, two new calls land on top. The first one is buried — and it may have been your biggest quote of the month.

The call is not missed. It is lost.

The job comes first. The calls are taken.

07Accessibility

Built for people who work alone

A traditional phone answering service charges by the hours it covers: the wider the window, the bigger the bill. That is logical, because there are salaries behind it. It is also why most self-employed people never had access to one.

We do not have that constraint. That is what lets us cover a full day — evenings, weekends and after-hours calls included — for someone working on their own.

08Questions from the self-employed

Phone answering for the self-employed: your questions

Do I have to change my number?
No. You keep your own and forward your calls whenever you want. You can switch back at any moment, without asking anyone.
What if I want to pick up myself?
Then you pick up. She only takes what you didn't: if you are free, nothing changes; if you are busy, the call is handled instead of lost.
Can she book an appointment in my diary?
Yes. She sees your real availability, offers slots that genuinely exist and books the appointment during the call. The caller hangs up with a date, not with a promise of a callback.
How do I handle time off?
You set it yourself, in two taps. She adapts immediately: no slots offered while you are away, and a real answer for the customers who call anyway.
Does she speak Dutch?
Yes. She speaks French, Dutch and English, and switches automatically into the caller's language.
Can I see what was said during the call?
Yes. Every call is transcribed, summarised and filed by reason in your dashboard. If there is any doubt, the exact conversation is there to read back.
I am a physio, an osteopath, a doctor — what about emergencies?
She does no medical triage: she assesses no symptom and takes no clinical decision. If you define transfer criteria, she applies them and puts the call through to the number you chose. The judgement stays human.
Where is the data hosted?
In Europe, in line with the GDPR. You can ask for it to be deleted at any time.
