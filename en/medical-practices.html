The situation

A phone menu doesn't answer: it queues you

Everyone knows the scene: a recorded voice, a keypad menu, then hold music. Two minutes go by, sometimes five. The phone menu has settled nothing. It sorted roughly, kept the caller waiting, then handed the call to the one person in the practice who was already busy.

And the missed calls always pile up at the same hours: on Monday morning the practice lines are saturated, and in the afternoon they ring into an empty room. Plenty of patients hang up before speaking to anyone and dial another practice. For a practitioner working alone, with no reception, it fits in one sentence: they are with a patient, so they don't pick up.

06Practice questions

Phone answering in a medical practice: your questions

Does she triage medical emergencies?
No. She applies the emergency criteria your practice has defined, and transfers the call immediately to the person you chose. No medical judgement is made by the machine: she assesses no symptom and takes no clinical decision.
Does this replace my receptionist?
No. It takes the phone off their hands so they can do the rest: records, reception, invoicing, insurance funds. Routine calls are handled and filed, and anything that needs their attention arrives with the reason and the summary already written.
Does she recognise a patient who has called before?
Yes. The history sits on the patient's record: when they called, why, and what was done. The conversation picks up with that context instead of starting from zero, and the practice receives that context together with the call.
How does it work with several doctors?
The assistant can be set up with the availability of each practitioner. She checks the right diary before offering a slot, and directs the patient to the right person based on the reason for the call. Everyone keeps their own hours, consultation types and rules.
Does it work with the Impulseo support schemes?
For GPs, all three Regions contribute towards the cost of a phone answering service, up to half of it. The exact conditions depend on your Region and your situation: raise it at the first meeting and we will point you to where to check.
Where is patient data hosted?
In Europe, in line with the GDPR. Conversations, records and call histories stay on European servers, and you can ask for them to be deleted at any time.
