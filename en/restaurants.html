The situation

Missed calls at exactly the hours the room is full

A restaurant phone has a talent: it rings at 12:30 and at 7:45 pm, exactly when the room is full and everyone is moving. Nobody here is employed to answer it. The owner picks up, or a waiter — so someone walks away from a table while two guests wait for the bill.

And it is never one call on its own. At peak time the line is already taken: the second caller gets an engaged tone, hangs up and books next door. The hours you get the most calls are the hours you can least afford to answer. That is not anyone's fault — it is the shape of the job.

04Accessibility

Cover for the hours that actually ring

A traditional phone answering service charges by the hours it covers: the wider the window, the bigger the bill. There are salaries behind it, so most restaurants cover nothing at all and the phone rings out through both services.

We do not have that constraint. That is what lets us cover lunch, dinner, late evenings and weekends — the exact hours when nobody in the room can pick up.

05Restaurant owners' questions

Phone bookings in a restaurant: your questions

Does she handle groups and large parties?
Yes, on your rule. Up to the threshold you set, she books automatically. Above it — ten covers, twelve, whatever you decide — either she takes the request and you call back, or she puts the call straight through to you. A large party commits your whole room, so the decision stays yours.
If I take a booking at the counter, does she know about it?
Yes, as soon as you block it in the table plan — a few seconds on your phone. The cover count updates and the assistant follows immediately: she will never promise a table that no longer exists. That is what stops double booking.
Does she note allergies and special requests?
Yes. An allergy, a vegetarian dish, a birthday, a high chair, a table on the terrace: the note is attached to the booking and you read it before the guests arrive. On a precise question about a dish she answers from your own information, or takes a clean message if it goes beyond that.
I already use booking software. Does this work alongside it?
Yes. Your online tool keeps working exactly as before. Celya only handles the phone, the channel booking software does not cover. You block in the table plan the covers taken through the other channel, and the two never step on each other.
Do I keep my restaurant's number?
Yes. You switch on call forwarding to the assistant, either permanently or only during service, when nobody can pick up. Prefer to keep the two apart? We can give you a dedicated booking number. Either way, you turn it off whenever you want.
