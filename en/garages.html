The situation

Missed calls from morning to night, and nobody to blame

Nine in the morning: a car up on the lift, a hose in one hand, the lamp in the other. The phone is in the office — fifteen metres, two doors and a pair of greasy gloves away. At the counter a customer is waiting for their keys, and a supplier is holding on the other line.

Mechanic, body shop, owner: everyone here has a trade, and answering the phone is not part of any of them. The ringing runs out, the driver hangs up and dials the next garage. That is not carelessness. That is what a real day in a workshop looks like.

05Garage owners' questions

Phone answering for garages: your questions

Can she give a price guide for an oil change?
Yes, if you want her to. You set the ranges you are happy to quote, and Janet gives them exactly as you wrote them. She never commits to a firm figure: for a quote she notes the vehicle and the request, and you confirm it yourself.
How does she tell an emergency from an ordinary appointment?
She listens to the reason, the way you would. Car immobilised, customer stranded, a shunt: that is an emergency, and she puts the call through to your mobile. A service to schedule or a question about price is an appointment or a message. The rules are set with you at install, and you can change them whenever you like.
What if I am already on the phone when she transfers a call?
If you do not pick up after a few rings, Janet takes the caller back. She reassures them, notes where they are, the vehicle and their number, then sends you the record flagged urgent. You call back as soon as you hang up, and the customer knows you have been told.
Does she know which makes I work on?
Yes. She is set up with your garage: the makes you accept, the jobs you do, your equipment, and what you do not handle. If a caller asks for something outside that, she says so clearly instead of booking an appointment you would have to cancel.
Do I keep my garage number?
Yes. You switch on call forwarding to Janet: permanently, or only when the line is busy or nobody picks up. Your customers dial exactly the same number as before, and you can turn forwarding off at any time.
